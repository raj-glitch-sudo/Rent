Settings

How your books are kept and what the app does for you automatically.

📒 Accounting basis

Your journal is recorded once. Cash-basis figures are worked out from it, so switching never changes your entries.

🤖 Automatic rent billing

On each boarder's due day the app posts Dr Tenant Receivables / Cr Rental Revenue. Payments you record then clear the receivable first; any extra is kept as Advance Rent and used automatically next month. Months before the start month are left as they are.

🧾 Accounts used by automation

Missing accounts are created for you the first time they're needed.

How can we help?

Search the guide, or pick a topic. Every answer is written for this app.

Popular questions

StartWhat should I set up first?
  1. Rooms — Property → Rooms & Inventory → + Add Parent Property, then + Add Room. Or import your Room Inventory tab (see Importing).
  2. Boarders — Property → Boarders → + Register Tenant, or import your Boarders Info tab. Set each boarder's rent and due day.
  3. Settings — check that Automatic rent billing is on and choose the month it should start from.
  4. Recurring bills — add internet, water, electricity and salaries once under Money → Recurring Bills.
  5. Back up — Settings → Download full backup. Do this every week.
StartWhat does the app do for me automatically?
  • Bills rent on each boarder's due day and tracks what they owe (Receivables).
  • Applies payments to the oldest unpaid rent first, and keeps overpayments as advance rent that is used on the next due date.
  • Drafts recurring bills every month so you only confirm the amount.
  • Suggests accounts for new entries from what you used last time.
  • Settles deposits when a boarder moves out (refund, apply to unpaid rent, or forfeit).
  • Locks closed months so finished books can't be changed by accident.
RentHow does automatic rent billing work?

On each boarder's due day, the app posts their monthly rent as a receivable: Dr Tenant Receivables / Cr Rental Revenue. The entry is called “Rent Due – NAME (Month)”.

It only bills from the start month in Settings onwards, skips any month that's already paid straight to revenue (for example imported history), skips ₱0 roommates in private rooms, and never bills inside a closed month.

Billing runs every time you open the app. You can also run it any time with ↻ Run rent billing on the Receivables page.

RentHow do I record a boarder's payment?
  1. Click ＋ New entry (top bar) or Pay next to the boarder on the Receivables page.
  2. Choose Income, then pick the boarder under Received From. You'll see what they owe, their advance and their deposit.
  3. Enter the amount and payment mode, then post.

The payment clears what they owe first (Dr Cash / Cr Tenant Receivables). Anything extra goes to Advance Rent and is used automatically on their next due date.

RentWhat do the aging columns mean?

Each unpaid rent bill is placed in a column by how many days have passed since its due date: 0–30, 31–60, 61–90 and Over 90. Payments are matched to the oldest bill first.

Use Statement to print a Statement of Account for a boarder, and Print aging for the whole list.

RentWhy does a payment show under a different month in the Payment Matrix?

The matrix places a payment under the month(s) it covers (for example the Month column of your imported Rent Transaction Log), not the day it was paid. A payment covering two months is split evenly between them. Entries without that information use their payment date.

RentA boarder paid ahead for next month. What happens?

Record it as a normal payment. Since nothing is owed yet, it is kept as Advance Rent (a liability — money you're holding). On their next due date, the rent is billed and the advance is applied automatically, so the month shows as paid.

BoardersHow are private rooms with several occupants billed?

A private room's rate is for the whole room. The first occupant gets the full room rate; roommates are set to ₱0 and are never billed. If occupants share the rent, set each boarder's rate to their share instead.

BoardersHow do I move a boarder out and settle the deposit?
  1. Go to Property → Boarders and click Move out on the boarder (or set their status to Inactive — the wizard opens).
  2. Choose the move-out date. The wizard shows unpaid rent, advance rent and the deposit held.
  3. Pick what happens: apply the deposit to unpaid rent, then refund, forfeit or keep holding the rest; write off any rent you can't collect.
  4. Review the entries and click Confirm move-out. Rent bills dated after the move-out date are removed.
BoardersWhere is a boarder's security deposit recorded?

Deposits are a liability — money you'll return — so they go to Security Deposits Payable, not income. Registering a boarder with a deposit posts Dr Cash / Cr Security Deposits Payable. It becomes income only if you forfeit it in the move-out wizard.

ExpensesHow do recurring bills work?
  1. Money → Recurring Bills → ＋ New recurring bill. Enter the name, site, due day, expense account, where it's paid from and the usual amount. Tick amount changes every month for water and electricity.
  2. Each month on the due day, the bill appears under Due for posting (and on the dashboard).
  3. Check the date and amount, then Post — or Skip if it doesn't apply this month.

Bills that already appear in two or more months of your history show under Suggested — one click turns them into recurring bills.

ExpensesWhy are accounts filled in by themselves?

When you type particulars you've used before (for example “PLDT”), the app copies the debit and credit accounts — and the amount, if empty — from your last entry with the same particulars. For new expenses it suggests an account from keywords like water, repair or internet. You can always change them before posting.

ExpensesWhat's the difference between a utility reading and a property bill?

Post Utility Reading bills a room or boarder for what they used and records the collection as income (Utility Revenue). Post Property Bill records the bill the provider (Bohol Waters, Bohol Light…) charges you, as an expense.

ImportHow do I import from my Google Sheet?
  1. In Google Sheets, click the header row, then Shift+click the last row and press Ctrl+C (⌘+C on Mac).
  2. Here, open Import & Export and click Rooms, Boarders, Rent or Expenses, then paste. The app recognises which tab you copied from its headers.
  3. Check the account matching and the preview, then click Import.

Import in this order: Room Inventory → Boarders Info → Rent Transaction Log → Expenses Log. Clear any filter in your sheet first — only visible rows are copied.

ImportWill importing the same rows twice double my numbers?

No. Rows with the same date, amount and particulars (or boarder) as an existing entry are flagged as Duplicate? and unticked. Boarders and rooms that already exist are updated instead of added.

CloseWhat does closing a month do?

Closing locks every entry dated in that month and earlier: nothing can be added, edited or deleted there, by hand, by import or by automation. That keeps reports you've already printed or filed from changing.

  1. Open Books → Month-End Close and click the month.
  2. Work through the checklist: rent billed, recurring bills posted or skipped, entries valid. Use Fix to jump to each item.
  3. Download a backup, then click Close. Earlier open months are closed with it.

Made a mistake? Reopen the latest closed month, correct it, and close it again.

CloseWhy can't I edit or delete an entry? (🔒)

The entry is dated in a closed month. Go to Month-End Close and click Reopen on the latest closed month (repeat to go further back), make your change, then close again.

ReportsCash basis or accrual basis — which should I use?

Accrual (recommended) counts rent as income when it falls due, even if unpaid, so your reports show what you've earned and what boarders owe. Cash counts income only when money is received.

Your journal is recorded once on accrual. Cash-basis reports are worked out from it (unpaid rent is held back until collected; advance rent counts when received), so switching never changes your entries. Choose Both in Settings to flip between them with the basis button in the top bar.

ReportsWhich reports are available?

Under Books → Accounting: the Journal, General Ledger (running balance per account), and Financial Statements — Trial Balance, Income Statement, Balance Sheet, Owner's Equity and Cash Flows — monthly, quarterly, year-to-date or custom, with comparison to an earlier period. Money → Receivables has the AR aging and each boarder's Statement of Account. All of them can be printed or saved as PDF.

ReportsWhy are entries listed newest first?

Your latest work is what you usually need to check, so the Data Entry feed, Journal, Ledger and Utility list show the newest entries at the top. The Journal and Ledger have a sort option if you want oldest first. Running balances in the ledger are always worked out in date order, whichever way it's sorted.

DataWhere is my data saved? How do I back it up?

Everything is saved in this browser on this device — there is no online account. Clearing the browser's data, or uninstalling the app, deletes it.

Download a backup every week: Settings → Download full backup (or Import & Export → Export Full System Backup). Keep the file somewhere safe, like Google Drive.

DataHow do I move my data to another phone or computer?
  1. On the old device: download a full backup (.json file).
  2. Send the file to the new device (Google Drive, Messenger, email…).
  3. On the new device: Import & Export → Restore System from Backup File, choose the file, and confirm.

Restoring replaces everything on the new device, so only do it on a device you haven't started using yet.

DataIt says “browser storage is full”. What do I do?

Download a backup first. Then remove large attachments (photos of receipts) from old entries — they take the most space. Settings shows how much space your data uses.

StartWhat's on the dashboard?

Revenue, expenses and net income for this month, year to date or all time (switch below the cards), unpaid rent, occupancy, the boarders who owe the most, and a Needs your attention list: bills to post, late boarders, months to close, deposits to settle and overdue tasks. Click any card to open the page behind it.

StartKeyboard and screen tips
  • Esc closes the pop-up that's open.
  • The « button at the bottom of the sidebar shrinks it to icons.
  • On phones, the ☰ button opens the menu.
  • The ☀️/🌙 button switches light and dark mode.

📖 Words to know

Receivable
Rent that's due but not yet paid.
Advance rent
Money paid before rent is due. Held until the due date.
Security deposit
Money held for the boarder and returned at move-out unless applied or forfeited.
Accrual / Cash basis
Count income when earned / when received.
Debit / Credit
The two sides of every entry. They're always equal.
Closed month
A finished month that's locked against changes.
